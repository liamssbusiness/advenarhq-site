How the missed call calculator works

You type four numbers: the calls you get in a week, the calls you miss in that week, what a typical job pays, and how many of the last 10 people you talked to hired you.

The math is simple. Missed calls a week, times your own booking rate, times your typical job. That gives the jobs and the dollars you likely lose each month and each year.

I do not count every missed call as a lost job. Missed callers are counted at the same rate as the people you do reach. The low and high numbers use a booking rate one notch lower and one notch higher, so you see a range and not one hopeful figure.
