Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

This policy explains how Jarvis, a personal AI assistant built and operated by Liam Schnorr under Advenar, accesses and uses data from Google APIs, including Fitbit data through the Google Health API.

Who this applies to

Jarvis is a personal-use application. It is used only by Liam Schnorr, with his own accounts. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from any other person.

What data Jarvis accesses

  • Gmail (liamss.business@gmail.com and advenarhq@gmail.com): reads messages to summarize and organize them, and drafts or sends messages only when Liam asks it to.
  • Google Calendar: reads the calendar list and reads and writes events, including a calendar named "Reminders", to track appointments and set reminders.
  • Fitbit, through the Google Health API (read-only): activity and fitness, sleep, and health metrics and measurements, to track fitness and wellness trends.

How the data is used

The data is used only to provide Liam's own assistant features:

  • Summarizing and organizing email
  • Drafting and sending email at Liam's request
  • Reading and managing calendar events and reminders
  • Tracking sleep, activity, and health trends

Data is never used for advertising, never sold, and never used to build profiles of anyone.

Where data goes

Data is stored on Liam's own computers (a MacBook and a personal home server). To produce summaries and replies, relevant content is sent to Anthropic's Claude API, and results are delivered to Liam through his private Telegram bot. These services only process requests for Liam. Data is not shared with anyone else.

AI and Limited Use

Jarvis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is not used to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine learning models.

Retention and deletion

Jarvis keeps only a small local cache (for example, recent email and calendar summaries) and the access tokens it needs to run. Liam can delete this data at any time by removing the files from his devices. Revoking Jarvis's access in Google Account settings stops all further access immediately.

Security

Access tokens and cached data stay on Liam's personal devices, which are not publicly reachable. Connections to Google, Anthropic, and Telegram use encrypted HTTPS.

Changes

If how Jarvis uses data changes, this page will be updated and the effective date will change.
