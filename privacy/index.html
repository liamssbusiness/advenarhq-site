Privacy Policy

This page has two parts. Part 1 is for people who use the Advenar website. Part 2 is the policy for Liam's private Jarvis assistant app.

Part 1: Advenar website visitors

Effective date: October 5, 2026

Advenar is run by Liam Schnorr in Tempe, Arizona. This part covers advenarhq.com, including the free Lead Response Test form and the Missed-Call Audit calculator.

What the website collects

  • The Lead Response Test form on the home page: your name, business name, cell phone number, website (optional) and trade. Only what you type into those boxes is sent, plus a hidden spam-check box that stays empty for real people.
  • The Missed-Call Audit calculator (advenarhq.com/audit): the numbers you type stay in your browser. The calculator does not send them anywhere.
  • Booking a call: the "Book a call" buttons take you to Cal.com, which is a separate service with its own privacy policy. Anything you enter there goes to Cal.com and Liam.
  • Email, calls and texts you send me directly are seen by me and my phone and email providers.

Why

To run your free Lead Response Test and to contact you about it. That is the only use.

Who sees it

Liam. When you send the form, your answers go to a small Cloudflare Worker that Advenar runs, and Liam gets a notification. The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads an animation library from cdnjs, so those services see your visit the way any web host does (for example your IP address). I do not run analytics on this site.

Not sold, not shared for marketing

Your information is not sold and is not shared with anyone for marketing.

Text messages

  • When you send the form, you agree to get texts from Advenar about your audit request.
  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to stop texts at any time. Reply HELP for help, or email advenarhq@gmail.com.
  • Agreeing to texts is not a condition of buying anything.
  • Mobile numbers are not shared with third parties for marketing.

Cookies and trackers

The site does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Delete your information

Email advenarhq@gmail.com and ask me to delete what you sent through the form, and I will.

Contact

Part 2: Jarvis app (private assistant)

The policy below covers a separate private application and is kept here unchanged.

Effective date: September 29, 2026

This policy explains how Jarvis, a personal AI assistant built and operated by Liam Schnorr under Advenar, accesses and uses data from Google APIs, including Fitbit data through the Google Health API.

Who this applies to

Jarvis is a personal-use application. It is used only by Liam Schnorr, with his own accounts. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from any other person.

What data Jarvis accesses

  • Gmail (liamss.business@gmail.com and advenarhq@gmail.com): reads messages to summarize and organize them, and drafts or sends messages only when Liam asks it to.
  • Google Calendar: reads the calendar list and reads and writes events, including a calendar named "Reminders", to track appointments and set reminders.
  • Fitbit, through the Google Health API (read-only): activity and fitness, sleep, and health metrics and measurements, to track fitness and wellness trends.

How the data is used

The data is used only to provide Liam's own assistant features:

  • Summarizing and organizing email
  • Drafting and sending email at Liam's request
  • Reading and managing calendar events and reminders
  • Tracking sleep, activity, and health trends

Data is never used for advertising, never sold, and never used to build profiles of anyone.

Where data goes

Data is stored on Liam's own computers (a MacBook and a personal home server). To produce summaries and replies, relevant content is sent to Anthropic's Claude API, and results are delivered to Liam through his private Telegram bot. These services only process requests for Liam. Data is not shared with anyone else.

AI and Limited Use

Jarvis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is not used to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine learning models.

Retention and deletion

Jarvis keeps only a small local cache (for example, recent email and calendar summaries) and the access tokens it needs to run. Liam can delete this data at any time by removing the files from his devices. Revoking Jarvis's access in Google Account settings stops all further access immediately.

Security

Access tokens and cached data stay on Liam's personal devices, which are not publicly reachable. Connections to Google, Anthropic, and Telegram use encrypted HTTPS.

Changes

If how Jarvis uses data changes, this page will be updated and the effective date will change.
